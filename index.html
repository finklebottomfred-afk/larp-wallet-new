About this wallet

Fictional wallet simulation. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any bank. Balances, account details, contact names, PayID references, transfers and receipts are fictional. Recipient names are entered manually and are not verified with a bank. The “Pay Now” button only updates this browser’s local demo balance; it cannot send money. Payment confirmations, screenshots, and shared receipts are not proof of any actual transfer. No bank or PayID network is connected.
